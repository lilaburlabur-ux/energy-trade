## 2. Company Overview and Recent Catalysts
TotalEnergies SE, an integrated energy company, produces and markets oil and biofuels, natural gas, biogas and low-carbon hydrogen, renewables, and electricity in France, the United States, Europe, Brazil, India, and internationally. Its Exploration & Production segment engages in the activities of exploration and production of oil and natural gas, as well as carbon storage. The company's Integrated LNG segment is involved in the upstream and midstream LNG activities, as well as biogas and synthetic methane activities, as well as gas trading.
